---
title: Card
description: A titled surface for grouping related content, metrics, and actions.
---

Card is a content panel with optional `title` and `actions`. `variant`, `color`, and `status` stay independent — the body copy stays readable while the surface carries the accent.

## Import

```tsx
import { Card } from "@harjs/react-ui";
```

```tsx
<Card
  title="Studio North"
  image={{ src: "/studio.jpg", alt: "Studio interior" }}
>
  Open workspace in Şişli.
</Card>
```

## Examples

### Variants

Card supports `filled`, `surface`, `surface-borderless`, and `outlined`. The default is `outlined`.

<Example>
  <CardVariants />
</Example>

### Colors

`color` tints the border and fill. Body text stays on the gray scale so nested content stays readable.

<Example>
  <CardColors />
</Example>

### Status

`status` adds a semantic accent bar. Use it for deploy, billing, and review states.

<Example>
  <CardStatus />
</Example>

### Actions

`actions` sits on the right of the title row. Pass one control or a short group.

<Example>
  <CardActions />
</Example>

### Metrics

Omit `title` and lead with a label, value, and delta for KPI tiles.

<Example>
  <CardMetrics />
</Example>

### Team

Cards can hold avatars, status chips, and a single action without extra layout primitives.

<Example>
  <CardTeam />
</Example>

### Pricing

Pair a highlighted plan with a quieter neighbor. `variant` and `color` do the emphasis.

<Example>
  <CardPricing />
</Example>

### Activity

A title, a live chip, and a stacked list. `Divider` separates rows inside the body.

<Example>
  <CardActivity />
</Example>

### With Image

Pass `image` with a `src`. `position` is `top` by default. `overlay` sits type on the photo. `start` and `end` place a portrait on the left or right.

<Example>
  <CardImage />
</Example>

### Form

Put fields in the body and keep submit in the card. `actions` holds a secondary control.

<Example>
  <CardForm />
</Example>

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | — |
| `title` | `string` | — |
| `actions` | `JSX.Element` | — |
| `variant` | `"filled" \| "surface" \| "surface-borderless" \| "outlined"` | `"outlined"` |
| `color` | Color or gray scale token | `"gray-500"` |
| `status` | `"primary" \| "success" \| "danger" \| "warning"` | — |
| `image.src` | `string` | — |
| `image.alt` | `string` | `""` |
| `image.position` | `"top" \| "overlay" \| "start" \| "end"` | `"top"` |
| `image.height` | `string \| number` | — |
| `image.width` | `string \| number` | — |
| `image.fit` | `"cover" \| "contain"` | `"cover"` |
| `border.radius` | `"0" \| "2" \| "4" \| "6" \| "8" \| "12" \| "16" \| "20" \| "40" \| "full"` | `"12"` |

`title`, `actions`, and `image` are optional. `height` applies to `top` and `overlay`; `width` applies to `start` and `end`.
